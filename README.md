## 🔬 What I care about

I’m interested in understanding language models as more than black boxes.

Most of the questions I find interesting live somewhere around:

* **Mechanistic Interpretability**
* **Computational Linguistics**
* **Language Model Behaviour**
* **Representations and Reasoning**
* **AI Evaluation**
* **Failure modes and generalisation**

I’m especially drawn to questions where the behaviour of a model looks simple from the outside, but the mechanism underneath turns out to be much more interesting.
